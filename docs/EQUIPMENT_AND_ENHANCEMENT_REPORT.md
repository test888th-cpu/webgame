## 2. Equipment categories

- **Permanent gear**: the six slots above; owned, saved, upgradeable copies
  (`PlayerProfile.own_copy`, `EnhanceRules`).
- **Temporary weapons**: street weapons picked up in a fight (`ground_weapons`, the
  `weapons` pool of an area: pipe, wrench, machete...). They break after their uses, are
  never saved, never enter the bag and can't be upgraded. The new lairs carry a small pool
  (pipe, wrench). The two systems don't share data.
- **Materials**: Scrap Metal and Refined Alloy only. No third material was needed: the
  item-level cost bands give the 50-70 sink.

## 3. Level 1-70 progression

- 1-50: unchanged gear, plus Core Spillway (43) gear: Spillway Chief's Coat and Visor (epic, Lv 42).
- 50-70, four tiers at 53, 58, 63 and 68 (Coolant, Underworks, Foundry, Heartforged), each with:
  - uncommon gear in all six slots, from the market;
  - rare gear in all six slots, from the ground's monsters;
  - epic gear in all six slots, from the lair boss, or rarely from the ground's elite.
- Top gear: legendary Slag Titan prizes (weapon, hands, charm, Lv 60) and the mythic Iron
  Sovereign set (all six slots, Lv 70).
- Stats are priced by the game's own budget (`ItemRules.budget`: (10 + 2·level) × slot
  weight × grade). The generator asks Godot for it (`ItemBudgetQuery.gd`) and scales the
  stat mix of the highest existing item of that slot and grade. So no stat is inflated past
  its budget, and `ItemRules.problems` stays clean.

## 4. Grade progression

| Grade | Budget multiplier | Source |
|---|---|---|
| common | 0.85 | starter kit |
| uncommon | 1.0 | market |
| rare | 1.15 | field monsters |
| epic | 1.35 | bosses, field elites (rare) |
| legendary | 1.6 | story and world bosses |
| **mythic (new)** | **1.85** | Iron Sovereign only |

Mythic has its own colour (`ItemText`, red `#ff6f6f`) and is preferred by the map's prize
display (`MapRules`).

## 5. Enhancement rules (+0 to +10)

These are unchanged: the cap is still +10 (no +11 to +15), the rates run from 100% down to
20%, pity adds 5% per failure, and a failure keeps the item and its level (there is no
destruction, so no protection item is needed). Upgrades are saved per item copy.

New: the cost grows with the item's level (`progression.json`
`enhance.item_level_costs`), while the rates stay the same:

## 6. Bonus tables

Each level adds 10% of the item's positive stats (penalties are never amplified):
+10 gives +100%. Example, Overdriven Gloves (`foundry_epic_hands`): at +10, every stat
is doubled. The workbench card shows exactly what the next level adds ("Next +N adds: ..."),
using `EnhanceRules.bonus_at`.

## 7. Material requirements

The table in section 5 lists them. For a full +0 to +10 run with one success per step:

Material counts round half up, with a minimum of 1 per step.

## 8. Material sources

- **Scrap Metal:**
  - 80% from every 50-70 monster;
  - 100% from the champions;
  - sold in the market's tier stock.
- **Refined Alloy:**
  - 22%, 25%, 28% and 31% from tier monsters;
  - 60% from champions;
  - 100% from every lair boss and from both new world bosses (so every boss guarantees
    material, as `test_materials` requires);
  - world-boss tiers pay 5, 8, 10 and 14, and a kill 18;
  - also in the market's tier stock.

## 9. NPC and UI

- **Workbench (city):**
  1. Pick the worn item.
  2. The card shows the current bonus, "Next +N adds: ...", the chance (pity included), the
     coins and materials, and what is missing.
  3. Confirm.
  4. The result toast and the saved level follow.

  This is the existing flow (`GameMenus`/`ItemCard`) plus the preview line.
- **Ama's market (city):** the tier stock opens when the previous lair boss falls; the shop
  says who brings the next stock ("Ama brings new stock once ... is defeated").
- **Uncle Thong's truck:** new "Rematches" heading for the lairs, beside Farming and World Bosses.
