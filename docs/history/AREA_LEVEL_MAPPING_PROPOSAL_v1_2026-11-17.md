## The rule

**The reference's own zone bands set the numbers.** Each zone file's
`levelRange` at `d2b91e08` gives:

| Reference zone | Band | Role |
|---|---|---|
| proving_shore | 1–2 | start |
| zone1 | 1–7 | levelling |
| farshore | 3–7 | levelling |
| zone2 | 6–13 | levelling |
| zone3 | 13–20 | levelling |
| realm, drakelands, frostveil, amberfall, willowfen | 15–20 to 19–20 | late levelling |
| evergarden, galecrest, nightbloom, palmreach, wraithwood | 20 | endgame |

**How our areas are fitted to them:**

1. Each story district takes the band of the reference zone at the same stage of
   progression (table below).
2. Inside a district, areas keep their current order and are spread over the band,
   so nothing becomes easier than the area before it. This is not a multiplier of
   old levels.
3. Side content (farming grounds, lairs, world-boss arenas, the arena) follows the
   story stage it belongs to: the last story area at or below its current level.
4. **At the cap.** The reference progresses at level 20 through dungeon and raid
   item levels, and through post-cap XP to virtual level 200 (RpgProgression).
   Cap content keeps its order as an **endgame tier**, which item levels will
   follow.

| District | Reference anchor | Proposed band |

| South Street | zone1 [1, 7] (start: proving_shore [1, 2]) | 1–7 |
| Port District | zone2 [6, 13] | 8–13 |

| The Black Network | realm [15, 20], drakelands [16, 20], frostveil [17, 20] | 18–20 |
| The Core Facility | level-20 zones (amberfall, willowfen, evergarden ...) | 20–20 |

## Conflicts with the reference progression

1. **Level cap:** `progression.json` max_level is 70; the
   reference's is 20, with post-cap virtual levels to 200. Character XP moves to
   RpgProgression (XP_TABLE, POSTCAP_GROWTH 1.1, prestige 23,200 per rank).
2. **Item levels:** 194 items have a level above 20. Under the adoption
   they are replaced or remapped to reference item levels and qualities
   (EQUIPMENT_AND_RARITY_CATALOG.md): endgame tiers give item levels, not
   character levels.
3. **Quest XP:** the quests pay absolute XP tuned to the old curve. Under the
   reference, quest and kill XP follow its rules (kill XP = 45 + 5 × mob level, with
   the level-gap scale). Quest XP needs the reference's quest XP rule to be
   verified first: UNKNOWN.
4. **Enemy levels** follow the area level (`LevelRules.enemy_level`). Their stats
   move to the reference mob formula (RpgMobs) with the adopted templates.
5. **Unlocks are unaffected:** every unlock is progress-based (`reached` /
   `defeated`), not level-based.

## Save migration plan (v13 to v14, not implemented)

- **Character level:** levels 1–20 are kept as they are. Above 20, the character
  becomes 20, with its lifetime XP converted to post-cap progress. **No XP is
  removed.** Total earned XP is kept, and the virtual level is computed from it
  (RpgProgression).
- **Area progress** (`visited`, `cleared`, `defeated`, unlocks): unchanged. Area
  ids do not change.
- **Items:** covered by the slot and rarity migration (EQUIPMENT_AND_RARITY_CATALOG.md).
  Unknown or retired items are parked, never dropped (the existing
  `test_item_safety` rule).
- **Testing:** every committed save fixture at v13 loads at v14, with the same
  areas, progress and items. There is a round-trip test, and a test that a v13
  level-70 save becomes level 20 with its full XP kept.
