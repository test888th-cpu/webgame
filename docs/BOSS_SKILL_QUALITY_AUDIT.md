# Boss Skill Quality Audit

An audit of the boss skill system, built on branch `claude/boss-skills` and integrated with
the five-job Player Skill system of `main` on `claude/boss-skills-integration` (uncommitted
work; see "Integration with the five jobs"):
- `client/data/boss_skills.json`, generated by `tools/content/boss_skills.py`;
- `BossSkillRules`, `BossSkills`, and the skill choice in `EnemyBrain`;
- the presentation in `GameFeedback`.

All 24 bosses and all 134 skills were examined: 110 regular skills and 24 Ultimates (one
per boss, added after the first audit; see "Ultimate skills"). Single-player only:
nothing here validates multiplayer behaviour.

## How it was audited

- **Source review** of the catalogue, the validator, the runner, the AI and the
  presentation.
- **A read-only measurement harness**, `client/tests/harness/BossSkillAudit.gd`:
  ```
  godot --headless --path client --script res://tests/harness/BossSkillAudit.gd
  ```
  - **Per skill:**
    - whether it lands on a hero standing in range;
    - whether a hero who reacts 15 ticks (0.25 s) after the warning takes nothing (three
      ways for a blow: roll away, roll timed into the blow, step out of its lane; from a
      hazard, walk to the nearest clear ground along the street or up and down the lane);
    - a combo's escape window;
    - the effect's drawn width against the hitbox;
    - the share of a hero's health it takes at the boss's level with four kits:
      - S0: starter kit;
      - B0: the shop's level gear, +0;
      - B5: the shop's gear, +5;
      - D10: the bosses' gear, +10.
  - **Per boss, two 90-second AI fights:**
    - one with a hero drifting about: skills used, repeats, the longest single attack,
      casts from out of range, and how much of the street around the hero waiting
      hazards cover;
    - one with a hero who only walks away from hazards: hazard hits against hazards
      landed.

    Halfway through each fight the boss is hurt into its later phases.
- **Screenshots in the real renderer (Xvfb, OpenGL),** with the live hitbox (red) and the
  waiting hazard areas (yellow) drawn over the scene. Representative cases: a strike
  (Furnace Baron), a combo (Big Sak), a charge (Tunnel Mother), a leap (Big Sak), a sweep
  (Slag Titan), a ground hazard (Sludge Foreman), a quake and a shock chain (Founder's
  Engine), a summon (Big Sak) and a phase-2 leap (Founder's Engine). The overlay script
  was temporary and is not in the repository.
- **Tests:** the existing suite plus 3 new tests (below).

Overall: the system is sound in the rules and in scripted simulation. Every boss skill is
real, owned, telegraphed, avoidable on reaction and cancelled correctly. Every boss has a
signature Ultimate that can kill a hero who ignores it, yet a scripted hero always
escapes it. Bosses fight differently from one another, and later bands add mechanics,
not just numbers. The one balance question found this round (P-1) was settled by the
owner: Founder's Engine's Ultimate waves were lowered from 50 to 46. No person has
played these fights yet.

- **`BossSkillRules._moves_taken`** (the validator's "no move shared" check) read one
  `move` per hero skill. Main's skills have passives (no move), later blows (`then`) and

- **`tools/content/boss_skills.py`:**

  passive). They now use the same measure as the generator, and the move-reuse check covers
  every hero move.
- **Nothing else.** Main's combat changes needed no boss change:
  - a boss's skill table (`{"move", "cooldown"}`, no `gauge`, `then` or `scale`) passes
    `UltimateRules.ready` and `SkillSequence` unchanged;
  - bosses cannot be held (`GrabRules`: shoved only; measured 0 ticks held in 1,080
    fights).
- **Boss data:** `boss_skills.json` is byte-identical to the original branch.
  - 24 bosses, 134 skill ids, 24 Ultimates, 163 boss moves;
  - every owner, reference, timing and damage unchanged (Founder's Engine's waves 46).
  - Main itself changed 24 of the 129 pre-job moves and retired some hero moves. Those
    are main's, kept as they are.

### P-2 (applied, owner-approved option 1): four boss skills raised to the new median

With the job kits, the heroes' median active skill measures 32 (was 29). The in-game rule
"a boss skill hits at least as hard as the heroes' median skill" (`test_boss_skills`,
`test_a_skill_is_a_bosss_blow_yet_fair`) fails for four skills by about one point:
- `SB_HARBOURMASTER_CHEN_002` and `MB_SECURITY_CHIEF_001`: 31;
- `WB_IRON_COLOSSUS_004` and `WB_SLAG_TITAN_003`: 29 × power 1.1, so 31.9.

The owner approved option 1, the smallest: +1 damage on each of the four, and nothing else.
- **What changed** (`SKILL_DAMAGE_OVERRIDE` in `tools/content/boss_skills.py`; the generator
  asserts the formula's value before overriding it, so a later formula change cannot be
  hidden):
  - Chen 002 and Security Chief 001: the combo finisher 19 → 20 (`bs_..._002_2` and
    `bs_..._001_2`). The light opener and its hitstun are kept, and the skill totals 32.
  - Iron Colossus 004 and Slag Titan 003: the hazard 29 → 30. The effective damage is
    31.9 → 33.0, because hazard damage is a whole number times base power 1.1, so 32.9 is
    not representable.
- **Scope:** exactly 4 damage values changed in the data.
- **Rejected options:** a full regeneration (39 entries, up to +10%), or accepting the
  four a point under.

### Five jobs against the 24 bosses (scripted simulation)

`client/tests/harness/BossJobMatrix.gd` (new, read-only):
- each job's hero fights each boss under auto battle (`AutoPilot`, the inputs a player
  presses), at the boss's level, shop gear +0, on a cleared street;
- 3 seeds, 360 fights per run;
- `NO_SKILLS=1` is the control (the boss without its catalogue, as on main before), and
  `FOOD=n` gives food.

| No food | Boxer | Brawler | Grappler | Counter | Striker |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wins, boss skills off (control) | 50% | 44% | 28% | 50% | 42% |
| Wins, boss skills on | 42% | 38% | 15% | 32% | 35% |
| Damage taken, % HP a minute (off → on) | 202 → 176 | 169 → 177 | 207 → 208 | 141 → 160 | 221 → 202 |
| Knock-downs a minute (off → on) | 5.0 → 6.4 | 6.1 → 7.8 | 5.9 → 8.0 | 4.4 → 6.5 | 5.2 → 7.7 |
| Longest stun, ticks (off → on) | 90 → 73 | 86 → 73 | 86 → 81 | 82 → 72 | 85 → 70 |

| With food (20 of each), as the game is played | Boxer | Brawler | Grappler | Counter | Striker |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wins | 97% | 96% | 96% | 89% | 96% |
| Median time to win (s) | 51 | 54 | 65 | 71 | 47 |
| Wins against level 56-72 bosses | 93% | 89% | 89% | 74% | 89% |

What this shows:
- **No stun-lock.** With boss skills, knock-downs rise, but the longest stun falls (at
  most 81 ticks, about 1.3 s).
- **No grab-lock.** Bosses hold nobody, and no boss was ever held.
- **Grappler** is the weakest job against bosses with or without boss skills (28% in the
  control). That comes from main's rule that bosses cannot be held, so its grab skills only
  shove them. Boss skills lower every job by a similar margin (the Grappler by 13 points).
- **Counter Fighter** loses the most with boss skills (50% → 32%; levels 30-55: 14/24 →
  5/24).
  - **Cause:** auto battle evades every telegraphed or guard-breaking blow
    (`AutoPilot._must_evade`). 89 of the 102 damaging boss skill moves are telegraphed
    (the fairness rule), so the stance is never tried against them, although 52 of them
    can be countered by the rules. Caught blows halve (559 → 286).
  - **Tested and ruled out:** making boss sweeps, or sweeps and charges, counterable
    changed nothing (identical results). The boss data is not the cause.
  - **Proposal (auto battle, not applied):** try a ready counter stance before evading a
    telegraphed blow it can catch. Measured in a scratch copy: Counter 23 → 35 wins of 72
    (levels 30-55: 5 → 12 of 24), Boxer 30 → 34. It also changes fights against
    ordinary monsters, so it needs main's full suite and the owner's approval.
- **With food**, every job beats every band; Counter is slowest and lowest late (74%).

### B. Hazard overlap

- **Before:** hazards the boss had called just before its Ultimate (a regular skill's,
  or its own hazard cycle's) could still be waiting while the first wave was marked.
  They could land in the Ultimate's safe ground.
- **Now:**
  - These earlier hazards keep their mechanics: they land where and when they were warned.
  - The Ultimate's first wave is marked only once they have landed
    (`BossSkills.clear_in`); the boss keeps channelling through the wait.
  - From the first tick of the cast until the last wave lands:
    - the boss's own hazard cycle and summons are paused;
    - the boss stands still and strikes nothing;
    - no regular skill is used.

  So nothing else can land among the waves, and dangerous pressure stays: the earlier
  hazards still land as warned.
- **Tests:**
  - `test_hazards_waiting_at_the_cast_land_before_the_first_wave`: all 24 bosses.
  - `test_nothing_else_lands_while_an_ultimate_is_channelled`: all 24 bosses; no other
    hazard, no blow, no summon, no movement.
- **Cleanup:**
  - When the boss falls, when it escapes at the bell, or when the hero falls, every
    waiting hazard and every unmarked wave is cleared (`BossSkills._stop`; tests for the
    boss falling mid-Ultimate and mid-cast, the escape, and the hero falling).

- **V-5, avoidable heavy blows.**
  - All 6 strikes, 8 sweeps, 14 leaps and 13 grabs can be avoided on reaction (a roll
    away, or a roll timed into the blow).
  - Grabs wind up for 18-24 ticks with the boss's glow and need the hero within 36 px.
- **V-6, the AI.**
  - In 24 fights, 22 bosses used every skill they have. Iron Sovereign (6 of 7) and
    Crucible King (5 of 6) each left one skill unused within the 90 s.
  - The same skill came twice in a row at most twice in 90 s, and there were no casts
    from beyond range.
  - The longest single attack was 110 ticks: a combo of three. Nothing got stuck in an
    attack or a cast.
- **V-7, chasing.**
  - A boss casts only within 260 px of the hero (a test checks every skill's range).
    Further off, it closes in at a stride faster than the hero's walk (`test_boss_chase`).
- **V-8, solid props.** A solid prop stops charges and leaps; the hero behind it takes
  nothing. ZoneSim's step rules apply to lunges (new test).
- **V-9, cancellation.**
  - **Boss death:** the boss's skills are removed and every waiting hazard is cleared.
    Nothing lands after the boss falls (test).
  - **World boss escape at the bell:** the same; no skill and no hazard after the bell
    (test).
  - **Hero death:** the same (new test).
  - **Scene transition:** each area gets a new `ZoneSim`, and the hazards, runner and
    brains live in it, so nothing carries over (by construction).
  - **Cooldowns:** they count down every tick on the fighter; they cannot stick.
- **V-10, phases.** A phase-gated skill is never chosen before its phase (test).
- **V-11, weapon wear.** Only moves from a weapon's own moveset wear the weapon (ZoneSim
  `_wear_weapon`), so boss skill moves never wear the boss's weapon.
- **V-12, the owner's rules.** No limit on HP food and no limit on world boss entries or
  attempts. Nothing in the skill system touches either.

### DEFECT

- **D-1, fixed: a combo kept swinging behind the boss's shield.**
  - **Reproduced:** Subject Zero started Zero Rend; it was then hurt into its shielded
    phase. Both follow-up blows landed while the boss was untouchable, although behind
    its shield a boss stands back.
  - **Fix:** `BossSkills` drops the rest of a combo when the shield is up (one line).
    Casts still land, because hazards run during the shield by design.
  - **Test:** `test_a_shield_drops_the_rest_of_a_combo`. It fails without the fix
    (2 blows) and passes with it.
- **D-2, resolved in the final round.** It was confirmed: with the original timings, 7
  charges stayed unavoidable even with a correctly timed roll. Charges and leaps now wind up
  at least 24 ticks, a charge stays active 12 ticks, and all 16 charges can be avoided on
  reaction (`test_boss_fairness`). See "Final validation", D.

### RISK

- **R-1, resolved in the final round.** Combo openers now wind up at least 18 ticks and
  light hits stun 6 ticks. Between blows a hero has 20-32 ticks to roll out (it was 14).
  See "Final validation", D.
- **R-2, hazard pile-ups.**
  - **For Ultimates: resolved** (see B). Nothing else is called during the channel, and
    earlier hazards land before the first wave.
  - **For regular skills: open.** Outside Ultimates, the boss's hazard cycle and its
    hazard skills can still wait at the same time, as designed.
  - **Measured:** the harness's simple evading hero, which never rolls or steps between
    waves, still takes some hazard hits in long fights (per-boss table).
  - **Next step:** a playtest.
- **R-3, damage with weak gear.**
  - **By kit:** the strongest skill per use takes:
    - S0 (starter kit at the boss's level): up to 67% of health (Founder's Engine,
      Piston Cascade, all three blows);
    - B0: up to 40%;
    - B5: up to 31%;
    - D10: up to 24%, and as little as 3%.
  - **Assessment:**
    - Starter gear at level 70 is not a realistic kit.
    - Shop gear stays under the 45% design cap, which a test enforces.
    - With boss gear +10, the lighter skills become minor. That is intended: gear is
      meant to matter.
  - **Spikes over 50% with S0:** four world boss skills (Founder's Engine ×3, Floodgate
    Marshal ×1).
- **R-4, effect placement depends on frame time.** A blow's effect is placed when a timer
  of its wind-up length (in seconds) fires. If the frame rate drops below 60, a lunge's
  effect lands where the boss was a few ticks earlier. This was seen in the capture
  harness, which ran the sim slower than real time. It is presentation only, and it also
  applies to hero skills.
- **R-5, quake warning at onset.** In the capture, the quake's crack warning was barely
  visible on its first frames; its landing burst shows clearly. Worth a look in the
  browser build.
- **R-6, combo opener already under way when the shield rises.** The opener still lands,
  as any plain blow under way does. Only the follow-ups are dropped (D-1).
- **R-7, multiplayer.** Not validated. Each hazard hits each fighter at most once, but
  target choice, shared pools and network timing need the server (WORLD_BOSS_DESIGN §4).

## Level 1-70 progression

Higher bands bring new mechanics rather than more damage:
- Bands 1-30 use only single blows, combos of two, crane drops and rows.
- Combos of three, volleys and summons arrive at 31-40; quakes and chains at 41-50.
- Phase finishers come at 51-70.

The median share of a shop-geared hero's health stays flat (18-20%) across all bands,
because difficulty comes from patterns. The shortest first-blow wind-up drops from 24 ticks
(band 1-10) to the 18-tick floor (bands 11-70), and the median telegraph tightens from 30
to 24 ticks. Nothing drops below the fairness floors.
