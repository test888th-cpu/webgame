## What changing it would mean

- 15 distinct Godforged items come from non-raid world bosses; 15 of them have
  no other source and would become unobtainable unless moved to another source.
- The first Godforged a player can earn today is at Lv 32 (a non-raid world boss);
  with raids only it would be Lv 56 (the lowest raid).
- Slots with Godforged only from non-raid bosses: none (raids cover every slot they do).
- Arena rival presets wearing these items: 6 slots (arena.json presets); the
  rivals' gear is a loadout, not a reward, and would not change.
- Players who already own these items keep them (saves hold item ids).
- The non-raid world bosses would need another top prize (Legendary gear or
  boss materials) so each keeps a reason to be fought.
